01 — The Menu

Crafted Drinks & Mocktails

Every drink is shaken to order and served chilled in our signature ROAM SOCIAL cups — built to travel, made to be photographed, poured to be remembered.

01 Classic

Old Fashioned

A timeless mix of bourbon or rye whiskey, bitters, and sugar.

02 Bold

Negroni

Equal parts gin, Campari, and sweet vermouth, known for its rich red hue.

03 Silky

Whiskey Sour

Bourbon, fresh lemon juice, simple syrup, and often an egg white for a frothy top.

04 After Dark

Espresso Martini

Vodka, coffee liqueur, and freshly brewed espresso for a sophisticated kick.

05 Spicy

Spicy Margarita

Tequila, fresh lime juice, agave, and muddled jalapeño for a modern twist.

06 Bitter

Boulevardier

A warm, bitter cocktail made with bourbon, Campari, and sweet vermouth.

07 Prohibition

Bee’s Knees

A Prohibition-era classic made with gin, fresh lemon juice, and honey syrup.

08 Celebration

French 75

Gin, lemon juice, simple syrup, topped with champagne for celebration.

09 Fresh

Mojito

White rum, fresh mint, lime juice, sugar, and club soda over crushed ice.

10 Mocktail

Basil Smash (Mocktail variant)

Fresh basil muddled with lemon juice and simple syrup, shaken with apple juice.

Small Plates & Bar Bites

Social Bites

Canapés built to travel from our mobile bar to your hand — plated fresh, finished on site, and designed to be picked up in a single bite between pours.

Seafood

Tuna Tartare on Crispy Wonton

Fresh, sushi-grade tuna diced and tossed with a light soy-sesame glaze, served on a crisp wonton square and topped with a tiny sliver of jalapeño or micro-cilantro.

Vibe Modern and refreshing; pairs beautifully with bright, citrusy drinks like a Margarita or French 75.

Pairs: Margarita • French 75
Vegetarian

Truffle Mushroom Arancini

Golden, bite-sized crispy risotto balls infused with earthy wild mushrooms and truffle oil, served with a dab of garlic aioli.

Vibe Rich, savory, and sophisticated comfort food that feels incredibly premium.

Beef

Filet Mignon Crostini with Horseradish Cream

Thinly sliced, rare beef tenderloin layered on a toasted baguette slice, topped with a dollop of sharp horseradish crème fraîche and sweet caramelized onions.

Vibe A hearty but refined bite that goes perfectly with bolder, darker drinks like an Old Fashioned or a Boulevardier.

Pairs: Old Fashioned • Boulevardier
Seafood

Smoked Salmon & Crème Fraîche Blinis

A classic catering staple featuring a tiny, savory buckwheat pancake topped with cold-smoked salmon, a touch of crème fraîche, and a sprig of fresh dill.

Vibe Timeless luxury that visually pops on a serving platter.

Gluten-Free

Prosciutto-Wrapped Asparagus with Balsamic Glaze

Tender-crisp asparagus spears wrapped in salty, cured prosciutto and lightly roasted, then finished with a drizzle of sweet balsamic reduction.

Vibe Simple, elegant, and easy for guests to pick up and eat without a napkin.

Sweet

Whipped Ricotta & Fig Tartlets

Miniature pastry shells filled with light, honey-whipped ricotta cheese and topped with a slice of fresh fig and a sprinkle of crushed pistachios.

Vibe A beautiful balance of sweet, creamy, and crunchy that adds a sophisticated aesthetic to the menu.

Zero-proof, always.

Every signature has a zero-proof variation, built with the same care. Custom menus for private lists on request.

04 — The Setup

The Mobile Bar

We take rugged, head-turning vehicles and rebuild them into high-end mobile catering units. A vintage white Mahindra Jeep carrying the ROAM SOCIAL name. A sleek, dark-themed bar under a black canopy strung with hanging bulbs. Steel, ice, citrus and craftsmanship — staged wherever the party happens.

Every setup arrives fully self-contained: bar counters, glassware, garnishes, ice wells and a bartender team that shakes to order on the spot. Private courtyards, rooftop terraces, weddings, corporate launches or brand activations — we bring the crafted drinks, the theatre of the pour, and a social experience your guests won’t stop talking about.
