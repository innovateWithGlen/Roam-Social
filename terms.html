8. Cancellation and Refund Policy

Booking Security and Advance Payment

To secure Roam Social’s services for a specific event date, a non-refundable advance payment (booking fee) is required at the time of invoice acceptance. Until this advance is received, the date remains open for other bookings.

Non-Refundable Advance

The advance payment is strictly non-refundable. This policy is in place because securing your event date requires us to turn away other potential clients for that same time slot, and covers the initial administrative, logistical, and procurement efforts required to prepare for your event.

Cancellation by the Client

If you must cancel your event, you must notify us immediately via email (support@roamsocial.in).

  • Cancellations made after the advance has been paid will result in the forfeiture of the entire advance amount.
  • Cancellations made within 7 days of the scheduled event date will require full payment of the remaining invoice balance, as perishable goods, staffing, and event logistics have already been finalized and committed to by Roam Social.

Rescheduling

If you need to postpone your event, we will make every effort to accommodate the new date, subject to our availability. The non-refundable advance may be transferred to the new date provided the request is made at least 14 days prior to the original event date. If we are unavailable on your new date, it will be treated as a cancellation from the client’s end.

Cancellation by Roam Social

In the highly unlikely event that Roam Social must cancel our services due to unforeseen circumstances, emergencies, or force majeure, you will receive a 100% full refund of all payments made to us, including the advance.
