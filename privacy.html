Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 15, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Roam Social. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you visit our website or enquire about our services. We are committed to maintaining your privacy and ensuring transparency in how your data is handled.

2. Information We Collect

We believe in minimal data collection. The only personal information we collect is the data you voluntarily provide to us when submitting an enquiry through our website’s contact form. This typically includes:

  • Name
  • Email Address
  • Phone Number (for WhatsApp communication)
  • Event Details

3. How We Use Your Information

When you submit a contact form, your details are sent directly to support@roamsocial.in. We use this information exclusively to:

  • Respond to your enquiries and provide quotes.
  • Communicate with you regarding your event booking via Email and WhatsApp.
  • Coordinate logistics for our beverage catering services.

4. Data Storage and Sharing

We do not store your data in external marketing databases, nor do we track your browsing behavior across our site. Once your details are routed to our support email for communication purposes, no other data about you as a user is stored on our servers. We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties under any circumstances.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out to us at:

Email: support@roamsocial.in
